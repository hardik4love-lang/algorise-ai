## Part 1: For Our Senior Client (Margaret's Easy Guide)
*Print this out and keep it by your telephone or computer!*

### 🌟 The 3 Traffic Lights You Need to Know:

1. **🟢 THE GREEN HEART SHIELD: "Everything is Safe!"**
   - **What it means:** This message is from your real doctor, hospital, pharmacy, grandchild, or friend.
   - **What to do:** You can safely read it, note your appointment, or pick up your prescription.

2. **🟡 THE AMBER SHIELD: "Check Before You Click"**
   - **What it means:** This is junk mail, sales ads, or an order confirmation.
   - **What to do:** Do not click on unfamiliar links. If it is a bank code, only use it if *you* just logged into your computer.

3. **🛑 THE GIANT RED STOP SIGN: "STOP! Do Not Answer or Pay"**
   - **What it means:** A scammer is pretending to be the Police, IRS, HMRC, or your bank.
   - **What to do:** 
     - **Hang up the phone immediately.**
     - **Do not send money or buy gift cards.** (Real police or banks will NEVER ask for gift cards or bitcoin!)
     - **Take a deep breath: You are safe.** No money has left your account.
